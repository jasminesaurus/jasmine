01 KISHVEEN farewell / advisory +

Bidding farewell to our seniors is hard, but saying goodbye to Kishveen? That’s like trying to spot her in a crowd, almost impossible, both because she’s tiny and because we’re not emotionally ready :’(

Our little (literally) bundle of joy, often affectionately known as Kishu, has been the heart of so many Invictus memories.

Whether it was playing the role of a kid in every single photostory and photography competition (a role she truly owned) or her iconic blue water bottle becoming a silent, ever-present member of our team, or her classic warning of ‘fielding lagegi teri’ that somehow made us smile, her presence was unforgettable.

You haven’t really known Kishveen until you’ve experienced the emotional rollercoaster of watching her steal your phone, deny it, then handing it back with a giggle like it was a gift. Elite menace and fully committed to the bit.

She could most often be seen charging up and jumping while trying to hit people twice her size, truly making her the most feared core member (shivering while typing ts)

As our Advisory, she consistently guided us with a calm demeanor and a clear vision. Her infectious cheer and unique quirks will be deeply missed.

Thank you for filling our days with unmatched energy and endless good vibes.

Team INvictus wishes Kishveen all the love and luck in this next chapter - may your adventures be as vibrant as you are <3
